## Data and knowledge

### Open data

- [Civic Data Atlas](https://civicdataatlas.org/) - Publishes Census demographic profiles for city council districts and neighborhoods in Chicago, New York City and Los Angeles. It is intended to help local officials, advocacy groups, journalists and residents understand who lives in each area.
- [Creative Commons](https://creativecommons.org) - An international nonprofit that provides legal and technical tools for sharing knowledge and culture. Its licenses and public domain tools are used by Wikipedia, Flickr, and millions of other creators and institutions.
- [Data Foundation](https://datafoundation.org/) - The Data Foundation is a non-profit policy research organisation that promotes open data and evidence-informed public policy. It conducts research, publishes analysis and convenes a coalition of data leaders.
- [Good Jobs First](https://goodjobsfirst.org/) - A national policy resource center that promotes corporate and government accountability in economic development. It maintains public databases on subsidies, tax breaks and corporate misconduct.
- [NYC Open Data Lab](https://martinezc1-nyc-open-data-lab.share.connect.posit.cloud/) - The NYC Open Data Lab is a public-facing research initiative that builds open-source tools and reproducible analyses from public data. It publishes student and collaborator work as civic data research and open educational resources.
- [Open Connectivity Foundation](https://openconnectivity.org/) - The Open Connectivity Foundation is a nonprofit standards organization that develops open specifications for secure interoperability between Internet of Things devices. It also maintains an open-source reference implementation and runs a certification program.
- [Open Contracting Partnership](https://www.open-contracting.org/) - A nonprofit that works with governments, civil society and business to publish and use data about public procurement. Its Open Contracting Data Standard helps make contracts more open and easier to monitor.
- [Open Geospatial Consortium](https://www.ogc.org/) - The Open Geospatial Consortium develops open geospatial standards, including OGC APIs, that let governments, companies and researchers share location data across systems. It brings member organizations together to keep that data interoperable.
- [Open States](https://openstates.org) - Publishes standardised legislative data for all 50 US states, Washington DC, Puerto Rico, and Congress through a website, API, and bulk downloads. It has been run by Plural since 2021.
- [OpenSecrets](https://www.opensecrets.org/) - A research and government transparency group that tracks money in US politics and its effect on elections and policy. Its website provides searchable data on campaign contributions, lobbying and political spending.
- [USAFacts](https://usafacts.org) - A nonpartisan civic initiative founded by Steve Ballmer that turns government data into free articles, reports and tools. It is privately funded and takes no advertising or external donations.

## Civic and community

### Civic tech

- [Alliance of Civic Technologists](https://www.civictechnologists.org) - A decentralized network of volunteer civic technology organizations across the United States. It connects local groups so they can share learning, support each other and improve how governments use technology and deliver public services.
- [Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org) - A non-profit digital encyclopedia of American politics covering elections, candidates, offices and ballot measures. It has 758,643 encyclopedic articles and says it has had more than 1.2 billion page views since 2007.
- [BetaNYC](https://www.beta.nyc/) - A New York City nonprofit that helps residents use civic technology, open data and design to improve their neighborhoods. It runs public programs, a data help desk and civic tools including Boundaries Map and the Urban Heat Portal.
- [Boston Area Research Initiative (BARI)](https://cssh.northeastern.edu/bari/) - The Boston Area Research Initiative is a research center at Northeastern University that uses civic data and community research partnerships to study social, economic and environmental issues in Greater Boston. It runs the Boston Data Portal and convenes researchers, public agencies and community groups.
- [Center for Civic Design](https://civicdesign.org) - An US non-profit that applies plain language, accessibility, information design and research to election materials and voter journeys. It publishes free tools, field guides and research for election officials.
- [Center for Tech and Civic Life](https://www.techandciviclife.org) - A nonpartisan nonprofit modernising US election administration through grants, research, training and data tools. Its projects include the Ballot Information Project and Governance Project.
- [Civic Infrastructure Collaborative](https://www.infrastructureforward.org/) - A Chicago nonprofit that works with public, private and civic partners on technology-enabled urban infrastructure projects. Its projects include electric vehicle charging, sensors and analytics for stormwater management, and capacity management in shared spaces.
- [Code for America](https://codeforamerica.org) - A US civic-tech nonprofit that works with governments to make public benefits and services easier to access, including SNAP, tax credits and criminal record relief.
- [Code for BTV](https://codeforbtv.org/) - A civic technology organization in Burlington, Vermont, that works with government and community partners on civic software and open data projects. It organizes volunteers to design, build and deliver tools for social and civic justice organizations across Vermont.
- [Code for Nashville](https://www.codefornashville.org/) - A volunteer Code for America brigade that works with community groups and Metro Nashville government to promote open data and build civic apps. Its projects include criminal record expungement tools, jail population reports and an online power of attorney form for immigrants.
- [Common Good AI](https://commongoodai.org/) - A nonprofit that uses AI and digital tools to help communities deliberate, identify shared priorities and make collective decisions. It runs participatory projects, including a multi-state Forum and a WhatsApp chatbot pilot for community needs assessment.
- [Democracy Works](https://www.democracy.works) - A non-partisan nonprofit that builds voter engagement and election data tools, including TurboVote, the Elections API, and the Voting Information Project.
- [Election.org](https://election.org) - A nonpartisan civic reference publishing source-linked records on US elections, candidates, elected officials, campaign finance and local government. It provides charts, downloads and documented data endpoints.
- [GovTrack](https://www.govtrack.us) - An independent project tracking US Congress legislation and votes plus White House actions. It publishes bill status, voting records, statistics and analysis.
- [ImpactLine](https://www.impactline.org) - A free public-budget reader that organises government revenue and spending with source links and fiscal periods. Its site covers the federal government, all 50 states and 35,395 local governments.
- [Metagov](https://metagov.org/) - A nonprofit research and infrastructure laboratory for online governance. It cultivates tools, research and a community of practice for people building self-governing digital institutions.
- [National Democratic Institute](https://www.ndi.org/) - The National Democratic Institute is a nonprofit, nonpartisan organization that works with local partners in more than 150 countries to strengthen democratic institutions and citizen participation. It runs a Democracy Innovation Lab and a democracy and technology program.
- [Open Cleveland](https://opencleveland.org/) - An all-volunteer civic technology group that works with local government and community organizations in Northeast Ohio. It runs hack nights and builds open data and web projects for the city.
- [OpenOakland](https://openoakland.org/) - A volunteer civic technology group that builds tools and data projects with Oakland public agencies and nonprofits. Its volunteers provide data analysis, software and design work at no cost.
- [SF Civic Tech](https://www.sfcivictech.org/) - A volunteer group of developers, designers, researchers and civic activists who build technology for San Francisco. It runs weekly civic hack nights and supports open-source projects that improve public services.
- [The Movement Cooperative](https://movementcooperative.org/) - A cooperative of independent organizations that shares data and technology infrastructure for progressive organizing. It helps members contact voters, mobilize supporters and coordinate their work.
- [Truth In Polling](https://truthinpolling.com/) - A nonpartisan 501(c)(3) nonprofit that runs a government accountability platform. It tracks legislation, campaign finance, voting records and government contracts for Congress and Indiana, with more states planned.
- [Vote.org](https://www.vote.org) - A nonpartisan nonprofit using technology and outreach to help Americans register, check registration, request mail ballots and find voting information. It says it has helped more than 40 million people get election-ready.

### Government technology

- [Beeck Center for Social Impact and Innovation](https://beeckcenter.georgetown.edu) - The Beeck Center for Social Impact and Innovation at Georgetown University works to build a people-centred, digitally enabled government, connecting government and the civic tech ecosystem through research, networks and practical tools. It was founded in 2014.
- [California Department of Technology (CDT)](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/) - The California Department of Technology is the state's central information technology agency. It oversees statewide IT policy, project delivery and security, and provides shared infrastructure and digital services to state government.
- [California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA)](https://cppa.ca.gov/) - The California Privacy Protection Agency is the state agency that implements and enforces California's consumer privacy law. It operates the Delete Request and Opt-out Platform and a data broker registry, and educates the public about privacy rights.
- [Coding it Forward](https://www.codingitforward.com) - A nonprofit that builds civic tech career paths for early-career technologists and places them in government agencies and nonprofits through its Fellowship.
- [InnovateUS](https://innovate-us.org/) - A partner project of several academic and public-interest technology programs that offers free online courses, workshops, and coaching on AI and innovation skills for public-sector professionals. Its courses cover responsible AI, public engagement, and practical uses of generative AI in government.
- [Open Mobility Foundation](https://www.openmobilityfoundation.org/) - The Open Mobility Foundation is a nonprofit that stewards open-source data standards such as the Mobility Data Specification and Curb Data Specification. Cities and technology partners use the standards to manage shared mobility and digitize public space.

### Nonprofit and NGO technology

- [Nonprofit Technology Network](https://www.nten.org/) - NTEN is a nonprofit membership network that helps nonprofit staff use technology effectively and equitably. It offers training, professional certificates, community groups and an equity guide for nonprofit technology.
- [Public Interest Registry](https://pir.org/) - A non-profit that operates the .ORG domain and related domain names for mission-led organizations. It provides those groups with a trusted online identity and works on internet policy and anti-abuse efforts.
- [Tangible AI](https://tangibleai.com/) - A social-impact technology company that helps social sector organizations use artificial intelligence, chatbots and automation. It also maintains open-source tools and runs an internship program for people entering pro-social AI work.
- [Tech Impact](https://techimpact.org/) - A nonprofit that provides technology services and training to other nonprofits. It also runs workforce development programs that prepare people for information technology careers.

### Crisis and humanitarian technology

- [Data Friendly Space](https://www.datafriendlyspace.org/) - Provides digital tools and data analysis for social impact and humanitarian organisations. It builds generative-AI tools for crisis response, including its GANNET suite.
- [Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT)](https://www.hotosm.org/en/) - The Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team is a global community that uses open map data and mapping tools to support disaster response, public health, displacement, and other humanitarian work. It works with local communities, governments, and organizations in 94 countries.

### Human rights technology

- [Awana Digital](https://awana.digital) - A non-profit that works with Indigenous and frontline communities to build technology for defending land, culture and human rights. Its tools include CoMapeo for territory monitoring and mapping, Terrastories for place-based storytelling and the Earth Defenders Toolkit.
- [Clooney Foundation for Justice](https://www.trialwatchtraining.org/) - The Clooney Foundation for Justice runs TrialWatch, a program that trains people to monitor trials and document whether courts meet international fair-trial standards. Its online training platform covers courtroom conduct, the right to a lawyer and trial delays.
- [Convocation Research and Design Labs](https://www.convocation.design) - A research and design lab that studies security technology and protects people and organizations facing harassment, surveillance, and disinformation. It works with human rights groups, journalists, and nonprofits.
- [Electronic Frontier Foundation](https://www.eff.org) - A nonprofit defending civil liberties in the digital world through litigation, policy analysis, grassroots activism and technology development.
- [Freedom of the Press Foundation](https://freedom.press) - A nonprofit that builds and supports secure journalism tools including SecureDrop and Dangerzone, runs the U.S. Press Freedom Tracker, and campaigns on press freedom.
- [Horizontal](https://wearehorizontal.org/index) - A non-profit that supports human rights defenders, activists and journalists with digital security and tool development. It builds open-source technology with at-risk users rather than for them.
- [Human Rights Data Analysis Group](https://hrdag.org/) - The Human Rights Data Analysis Group is a non-profit, non-partisan organization that applies statistics and data science to the analysis of human rights violations. It works with truth commissions, courts, and human rights groups to build evidence for accountability.
- [Open Technology Fund](https://www.opentech.fund/) - The Open Technology Fund is an independent nonprofit that funds and supports technology projects countering online censorship and surveillance. It provides grants, security audits and other help to internet freedom projects.
- [Public Knowledge](https://publicknowledge.org) - A policy nonprofit working on freedom of expression, an open internet, affordable communications and creative works. Its current issues include broadband access, copyright reform and trustworthy AI.
- [Security Force Monitor](https://securityforcemonitor.org/) - A research group that compiles public records into a searchable database of security force units, commanders and areas of operation. Human rights researchers, litigators and journalists use the data to investigate alleged abuses and hold security forces accountable.

### Legal aid and justice technology

- [Free Law Project](https://free.law/) - A non-profit that uses software, data and advocacy to make legal information more accessible. It runs CourtListener, the RECAP archive and related tools for public access to court records.
- [Measures for Justice](https://measuresforjustice.org/) - A nonpartisan nonprofit that helps communities and criminal justice agencies collect, standardize, and use data to make the system more transparent and accountable. It publishes a National Data Portal and works directly with local justice agencies.

### Journalism and media technology

- [BillBoard](https://mybillboard.org/) - A Maryland nonprofit that tracks US Congress legislation and publishes nonpartisan, source-linked explanations of bills, hearings and official actions. It provides reporting and tools intended to help everyday voters understand what legislation would do and who it could affect.
- [Knight Lab](https://knightlab.northwestern.edu/) - A community of designers, developers, students, and educators at Northwestern University that builds open-source tools for journalism and storytelling. Its tools include TimelineJS, StoryMapJS, and JuxtaposeJS, and it runs an interdisciplinary studio class.
- [Project Geospatial](https://projectgeospatial.org/) - A non-profit media organization that publishes articles, podcasts and event coverage about geospatial technology. It works to make geospatial knowledge and career information more accessible to students, professionals and the public.
- [ProPublica](https://www.propublica.org) - An independent, non-profit investigative newsroom producing public-interest journalism. Its investigations are published free online and often include datasets, tools and legal action to obtain records.

## Access and inclusion

### Digital inclusion

- [CyPurr Collective](https://cypurr.org) - A grassroots group that runs beginner-friendly digital security workshops and cryptoparties in New York City. It publishes its training materials under an open license so others can reuse and adapt them.
- [EveryoneOn](https://www.everyoneon.org) - A US digital inclusion nonprofit connecting under-resourced communities to affordable internet, computers and digital skills training, with place-based work in California and Milwaukee.
- [Kapor Center](https://www.kaporcenter.org) - A family of organisations, including Kapor Foundation, SMASH, Kapor Capital, Kapor Advocacy and the P.R.I.M.E. Alliance, working to remove barriers for people of colour in tech through research, programmes, advocacy and investment.

### Disability and accessibility technology

- [American Foundation for the Blind](https://www.afb.org) - The American Foundation for the Blind is a US nonprofit expanding opportunities for people who are blind or have low vision through advocacy, research, accessible technology and professional services. It was founded in 1921.
- [Knowbility](https://knowbility.org) - An Austin-based nonprofit that runs accessibility training, consulting and usability testing, including the Accessibility Internet Rally and the John Slatin AccessU conference. It was founded in 1999.

### Education equity technology

- [Benetech](https://benetech.org) - A nonprofit building accessible education technology, including Bookshare and its Global Certified Accessible programme. It campaigns to make accessibility the norm in schools and institutions.
- [CodeAI](https://code.org) - An education nonprofit, formerly Code.org, working so every K-12 student can learn AI and computer science. It provides free curriculum, teacher training and policy work, and says it has reached 155 million students and 3 million teachers in 190 countries.
- [ConsiliumBots](https://www.consiliumbots.com) - A nonprofit that builds virtual assistants and personalized recommendations from government data. Its tools help people make decisions about education, housing, and public benefits.

## Health and wellbeing

### Mental health technology

- [Crisis Text Line](https://www.crisistextline.org) - Provides free, 24-hour, confidential text support for people in crisis. It says it has supported over 11 million conversations in the US since 2013.

## Climate, food and housing

### Climate technology

- [Air Alliance Houston](https://airalliancehouston.org) - A non-profit advocacy organization working to reduce the public health impacts of air pollution and advance environmental justice in Houston. It runs AirMail, a system that scans state air permit applications and notifies nearby residents, and publishes air monitoring and pollution data.
- [CarbonPlan](https://carbonplan.org) - A non-profit that analyses climate solutions, focusing on carbon offsets, carbon removal, and climate risk. It publishes open data and tools including OffsetsDB and Open Climate Risk.
- [Rewiring America](https://www.rewiringamerica.org) - A US nonprofit working to electrify households and make clean energy affordable, through policy, data tools and community programmes.

### Food rescue and food security

- [Feeding America](https://www.feedingamerica.org) - The largest hunger-relief organisation in the United States, a national network of 250+ food banks, 20+ state associations and 60,000+ agency partners. It distributes food and advocates on hunger policy.

### Housing and homelessness technology

- [Airbnb.org](https://www.airbnb.org) - A non-profit that provides free emergency housing to people affected by disasters, illness or other crises. It works with partner organizations and uses Airbnb's platform and host network to arrange short-term stays.
- [Community Solutions](https://community.solutions) - A nonprofit working with more than 150 cities and counties through Built for Zero to reduce homelessness using real-time data and coordinated action.

## Work, money and learning

### Worker and platform co-ops

- [U.S. Federation of Worker Cooperatives](https://usworker.coop) - The U.S. Federation of Worker Cooperatives is the national grassroots membership organisation for worker cooperatives and democratic workplaces. It says its members represent an estimated 1,300 US worker co-ops and their 15,000 workers.

### Research and education technology

- [Aquaya](https://aquaya.org) - A nonprofit research and consulting organization that works to improve water, sanitation and hygiene access in low- and middle-income countries. It collects and analyzes WASH data and builds tools to inform policy, programme and financing decisions.
- [Berkeley Institute for Data Science](https://bids.berkeley.edu) - A research institute at UC Berkeley that supports data-intensive research, open-source software and data science training. It runs programs in open scholarship, AI and science, and hosts events and training for researchers across disciplines.
- [Code for Science & Society](https://www.codeforsociety.org/) - A US public charity and fiscal sponsor for public-interest technology, data and research projects. It provides financial, operational and strategic support to open-source and open-data initiatives.
- [Community Data Science Collective](https://wiki.communitydata.science/Main_Page) - The Community Data Science Collective is an interdisciplinary research group of faculty and students at several US universities who study online communities and collaborative production. Its members use quantitative and qualitative methods to examine how communication and information technologies shape participation and public goods.
- [Cosmik](https://cosmik.network) - Builds open tools and networks for collective sensemaking, including a social knowledge network for researchers. It uses AI and decentralized publishing protocols such as ATProto to make research contributions discoverable and reusable.
- [DataKind](https://www.datakind.org) - Works with social impact organisations to design data science and AI projects, and builds shared products such as its Beacon community data platform. It also runs the Data for Good movement and education initiatives.

## Tech ethics and responsible AI

### Technology ethics and responsible AI

- [AI Now Institute](https://ainowinstitute.org) - An independent research institute producing policy research on AI's social consequences, corporate power, surveillance and public accountability.
- [AI Objectives Institute](https://ai.objectives.institute) - A non-profit research and development lab working on how AI and economic systems can reflect human objectives. Its team builds tools and conducts research on AI governance, markets and structural risks.
- [AI Risk and Vulnerability Alliance](https://avidml.org/arva) - A non-profit that runs AVID, a database and toolset for reporting and managing vulnerabilities in AI systems. It works with volunteer engineers and researchers to document AI failure modes and support risk management.
- [Berkman Klein Center for the Internet and Society](https://cyber.harvard.edu/) - The Berkman Klein Center for the Internet and Society is an academic research center at Harvard University that studies the internet and digital society. It builds tools and platforms and runs fellowships and public programs on the internet, law and digital policy.
- [Center for AI and Digital Policy (CAIDP)](https://www.caidp.org/) - The Center for AI and Digital Policy is a global nonprofit based in Washington, DC that works on AI governance grounded in democratic values, human rights and the rule of law. It publishes an annual AI policy index and runs a policy clinic, fellows program and public events.
- [Center for Digital Democracy](https://democraticmedia.org/) - The Center for Digital Democracy is a research and advocacy organization that works on digital privacy, consumer protection and the accountability of technology platforms. Its work covers digital marketing, data practices and the effects of technology on young people and democratic processes.
- [Center for Informed Democracy and Social Cyber-Security (IDeaS)](https://www.cmu.edu/ideas-social-cybersecurity/) - The Center for Informed Democracy and Social Cyber-Security is a Carnegie Mellon University research center that studies disinformation, hate speech and extremism online. It runs research, education and community programs intended to help people understand and resist online manipulation.
- [CivAI](https://civai.org/) - A nonprofit that educates the public about the capabilities and dangers of AI through interactive software demonstrations. It also gives briefings to government and civil society groups.
- [Current AI](https://www.currentai.org) - A global nonprofit partnership that funds and builds open, public-interest AI infrastructure. It brings governments, philanthropists, researchers, and grassroots builders together around the Paris Charter on AI in the Public Interest.
- [Data & Society Research Institute](https://datasociety.net) - Data & Society is an independent nonprofit research institute studying the social implications of data, automation and AI, producing research to ground public debate and technology policy.
- [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/) - A non-profit research institute studying the trajectory of artificial intelligence. It publishes independent data, benchmarks and analysis on AI capabilities, compute and impacts.
- [Humane Intelligence](https://humane-intelligence.org/) - A 501(c)(3) nonprofit that designs and runs evaluations and red-teaming exercises to make AI systems more accountable, responsible, and fair. It also runs bias bounties, working groups, and volunteer programs.
- [Institute for Security and Technology](https://securityandtechnology.org/) - The Institute for Security and Technology is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that brings technologists, policymakers, and industry leaders together to develop practical responses to emerging security challenges. Its work covers cybersecurity, artificial intelligence risk, critical infrastructure, and nuclear command and control.
- [Mozilla Foundation](https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/) - The Mozilla Foundation is a nonprofit that funds and supports people building a more open and accountable internet. Its work includes fellowships, grants, Mozilla Festival and Common Voice, a public speech dataset project.
- [Public AI Inference Utility](https://publicai.co/) - A nonprofit, open-source project that runs a shared access point for public and sovereign AI models. It also advocates for AI as public infrastructure rather than a privately controlled service.
- [REAL ML](https://realml.org/) - A non-profit network that connects and resources people working on AI and algorithmic accountability. It runs workshops, a fellowship and a global community focused on the experiences of marginalized communities.
- [TechTonic Justice](https://www.techtonicjustice.org/) - A nonprofit that works with low-income communities, lawyers and organizers to resist harmful uses of artificial intelligence. It provides training, legal support and research on automated decisions in areas such as benefits, housing and education.
- [Twin Cities Innovation Alliance](https://www.tciamn.org/) - A Minneapolis social venture that supports entrepreneurs and community leaders from Black, Indigenous and other marginalized communities. It runs initiatives on ethical AI, data transparency and algorithmic justice, including the NOTICE Coalition against youth surveillance in schools.

### Words used in this list

**API**: a way for one computer program to ask another for data.

**GIS**: software for making and reading maps from data.

**AI**: software that can do tasks that usually need human judgement.
